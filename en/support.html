Frequently asked questions

How do I delete my account?
In the app, go to Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account, or send us your request by email. After a deletion request, your data is removed within the period specified in the Privacy Policy.

I forgot my password, what do I do?
Use the "Forgot Password" link on the sign-in screen to get a reset link sent to your email.

I saw inappropriate content, how do I report it?
Use the "Report" option in the "..." menu on the post. Our moderation team reviews reports promptly.

I want to report a bug.
Email the issue (including a screenshot if you have one) to info@startyazilim.com.

Response time

We typically respond to support requests within 1-2 business days.
