Features

Everything you need to speak your mind

Stay hidden while your voice gets heard. Every GossiGo feature is built around privacy and fun.

100% Anonymous

No post is ever tied to your real identity. Speak freely — no account photo or real name required.

Private Groups

Start closed groups with your friends, coworkers, or classmates — gossip stays inside that circle.

Live Trending

The most-talked-about posts rise in real time. Never miss what's blowing up.

Reactions & Engagement

Vote, comment, react. Posts naturally "age" over time and fade into the archive.

Safe Moderation

Reporting and moderation tools keep the community clean; harmful content is removed quickly.

You Stay in Control

Delete your own posts anytime, leave a group, or close your account completely — whenever you want.
